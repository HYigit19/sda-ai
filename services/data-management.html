Data Management Services for AI: Integration, Quality & Governance

Most AI projects that stall do so because of data, not models. Customer records exist three times, supplier names are spelled five ways, and the documents an assistant needs are scattered across drives and inboxes.

We bring your data into shape: we connect the sources, clean and deduplicate records, define who owns what, and build pipelines that keep everything current. The result is a reliable foundation for AI agents, automation and reporting.

What we deliver

Choose the parts you need. We can deliver a complete solution or work alongside your own team.

Data integration

Reliable pipelines from ERP, CRM, databases, files and APIs into one governed platform.

Data quality & cleansing

Find and fix duplicates, gaps and inconsistencies, with AI-assisted matching.

Master data management

One trusted record for customers, suppliers, materials and products.

Knowledge bases for AI

Prepare documents and records for retrieval, with vector search and access rules.

Governance & access

Ownership, data catalog, lineage and role-based access, documented and auditable.

Reporting foundation

Clean, modelled data that feeds your BI tools and management reports.

Common questions

Why does data quality matter for AI?

AI agents and forecasts repeat whatever is in your data. Duplicates, gaps and outdated records lead directly to wrong answers and bad decisions.

Do we need a data warehouse first?

Not necessarily. We start with the data the first use case needs and grow the platform when volume or complexity requires it.

How do you handle sensitive data?

We apply role-based access, encryption and masking, and keep data in the environment you choose.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We build on the databases, cloud platforms and BI tools you already use wherever that makes sense.
