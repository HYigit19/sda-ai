SAP AI Integration, SAP BTP Extensions & ERP Integration

Your ERP holds the data that makes AI useful: orders, materials, suppliers, quality records and customers. The challenge is connecting to it securely without modifying the core system.

We build side-by-side extensions on SAP BTP and integration layers that expose the right data to AI applications through governed APIs. That keeps your SAP core clean and upgrade-safe.

Common questions

Can you connect AI to SAP?

Yes, we connect AI to SAP and other ERP systems through secure, approved APIs.

Will the integration disrupt daily operations?

No. We build alongside your existing systems and roll out in steps, with testing before anything goes live.

Can AI write data back into our systems?

Yes, with permission checks, and with a human approval step for critical transactions.

Which other systems can you integrate?

CRMs, document management, databases and most systems with an API, including older file-based interfaces.
