Custom Web Application Development with AI

Off-the-shelf software rarely fits how your business actually works. A custom web application can match your process exactly, connect to your existing systems and include AI features where they add real value.

We take a product from first concept through design, development and launch, with automated tests, CI/CD and documentation so your team or ours can keep improving it.

What we deliver

Choose the parts you need. We can deliver a complete solution or work alongside your own team.

Product discovery

User interviews, scope and a clickable prototype before development starts.

UX/UI design

Clean, accessible interfaces that follow WCAG and your brand.

Full-stack development

TypeScript frontends, robust APIs and well-modelled databases.

AI features

Search, summaries, document upload, recommendations and assistants inside your app.

Customer & partner portals

Secure self-service portals with single sign-on and role-based access.

DevOps & hosting

CI/CD pipelines, monitoring, backups and cloud infrastructure as code.

Common questions

How long does it take to build a web application?

A focused MVP usually takes 6 to 12 weeks. We deliver in two-week sprints so you see working software early and can adjust priorities.

Who owns the code?

You do. All source code, designs and documentation are transferred to you.

Can you take over an existing application?

Yes. We start with a code and architecture review, then stabilise and extend the application.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance and further development packages with agreed response times.
