Mobile App & Field Service App Development

Field teams still lose time with paper forms, phone calls and re-typing data back at the office. A well-designed mobile app removes that work and gives managers a live view of what is happening.

We build cross-platform apps that are simple enough to use with gloves on, work without a signal and connect to your ERP, CRM or maintenance system. AI features such as photo recognition and voice notes speed up reporting further.

Common questions

Native app or progressive web app?

A progressive web app is faster and cheaper to roll out. Native or cross-platform apps make sense when you need offline mode, camera features or app store distribution. We advise per project.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. We design offline-first, so data is stored on the device and synced when a connection returns.

Can the app connect to our ERP?

Yes, we connect it to your ERP and other business systems.

How do you handle data protection?

Data is encrypted on the device and in transit, and we follow GDPR requirements for location and photo data.
